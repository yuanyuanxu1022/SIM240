# F1 Figure description


## Figure 1
F1_resistance_vs_height.png

Effective hydraulic resistance as a function of liquid film height.

Parallel and perpendicular transport directions are compared.
The resistance decreases with increasing liquid height because the relative confinement effect decreases.


## Figure 2
F1_anisotropy_ratio.png

Resistance anisotropy ratio:

R_T/R_P

The ratio decreases with increasing film height.
The result indicates that nanoscale groove-induced directional resistance is strongest under strong confinement.
