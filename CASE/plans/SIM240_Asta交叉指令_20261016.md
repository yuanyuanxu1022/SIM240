## 主问题（先贴这条）

> Has anyone quantitatively compared an analytical effective-permeability approximation (such as the local cubic law, or stripe/arithmetic–harmonic averaging) against an exact or high-fidelity numerical solution for **laminar, single-phase flow in channels with rectangular grooves or ridges under a finite gap**? I am specifically looking for studies that report **numerical error** of the approximation (not just derivation or application), and ideally state under what aspect-ratio / gap conditions the approximation breaks down.

（中文大意：有没有人做过「矩形沟槽 + 有限间隙、层流单相」下，解析有效渗透率近似——如局部立方律、条纹算术/调和平均——与精确/高保真数值解的**定量误差对比**？我要的是报了近似**数值误差**、最好还给出它在什么高宽比/间隙下失效的研究，不是只有推导或应用。）

## 补充问题（可按需分别再提交一次）

1. **纳米压印方向**：
> In nanoimprint / UV-NIL template-filling simulation, has an anisotropic permeability or effective-transport model been **validated** against a full numerical simulation? I am asking about validation studies (quantitative comparison), not papers that simply use a permeability model.

2. **微流控/润滑方向**：
> For corrugated or grooved microchannels, at what gap-to-period or groove aspect ratio does the lubrication approximation (or local cubic law / Reynolds equation with a flow-factor correction) start to fail, and is this failure quantified numerically? Please cite any work that gives a quantitative breakdown or error bound.

3. **等价公式的别名（防漏）**：
> Are there other names for the "local cubic law" or "stripe-averaged permeability" for flow over grooves — such as flow-factor methods, Patir–Cheng models, or equivalent-slip / effective-height corrections — and has any of them been quantitatively benchmarked against a direct numerical solution for rectangular-groove geometry?

## 从严判定口径（拿到结果后按此分级，不要自动往"空白"上靠）

任何命中，先落到下面三档之一，再决定措辞：

- **直接先例**：几何同为矩形/直角沟槽 + 有限间隙，闭合同为（或等价于）局部立方律/条纹平均，且给出了**定量误差**。
- **近邻先例**：周期尺寸不同、相邻几何（平滑/三角/其他带角形状）、极限理论（无限长槽、零间隙等），或**同一规律的等价命名**（flow-factor / Patir–Cheng / 等效滑移 / 有效高度）。**不得因"不是相同纳米尺寸"就排除。**
