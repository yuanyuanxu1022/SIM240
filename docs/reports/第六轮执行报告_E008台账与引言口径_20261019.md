### 2.2 引言恒等关系口径（第 48 行正文 + 第 169 行写作说明）

**第 48 行**：
- 原：`...under the four conditions listed in Sec. II it is the same object, and for a square wave the identity is exact.`
- 新：`...under a common definition of the variables, the two expressions are algebraically identical, and for a square wave the identity is exact. **Whether the underlying Reynolds approximation is physically adequate for the present geometry is a separate question, examined in Sec. II and quantified in Sec. III.**`

**第 169 行写作说明**同步改为「under a common definition of the variables **the two expressions are algebraically identical**」。

现在全稿口径统一为：**共同定义 → 代数恒等；Reynolds 近似是否充分 → 另一个待检验问题**（第1章 48/181、第2章 240/258 一致）。

### 2.3 体素段首句改为条件句（第 2 章 116/144 行）

**英文**：
- 原：`The alternative, a staircase (voxel) representation, in general places the wall on the nearest node rather than half a cell away. If the snapping displaced every surface by the worst-case Δx/2=2.5 nm, that would be...`
- 新：`**Consider a geometric discretization or wall-placement strategy under which the normal position of a surface is displaced by as much as** Δx/2=2.5 nm. At that displacement the error would be 3.3% in h and 2.5% in d...`

**中文**：
- 原：`若改用阶梯（体素）表示，壁面一般会被放到最近节点而非半格处。若该吸附使所有表面偏移...`
- 新：`**考虑某种几何离散或壁面放置策略，其表面法向位置偏移可达** Δx/2=2.5 nm。在这一偏移量下，...`

- 段尾既有的「未分析具体体素实现、不是实测对比」限定保留。
- 不再出现"体素一定会怎样"的事实判断。
