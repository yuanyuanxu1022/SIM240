### ⚠ 一个必须避开的"通用建议"陷阱

网上有中文投稿攻略建议："摘要结尾需明确标注 `To the best of our knowledge, this is the first study to...` 以凸显原创性"。

**这条对我们有害，绝对不做。** 理由：查新任务 C（WoS 被引列表）未完成，"首次"在我们项目里是**禁写词**（见范围文档 §1 与引言草稿的措辞纪律）。照抄这句话等于把两轮查新的成果一次性作废。

## 二、Front matter（可直接用）

### 2.1 Title

**推荐**：

> **Systematic error of the arithmetic–harmonic permeability closure for directional transport in rectangular imprint grooves**

**备选 A**（更强调定量）：

> **How much does the stripe-average closure err in rectangular imprint grooves? A direct numerical answer over 36 geometries**

**备选 B**（更强调可移植性）：

> **A verified two-factor correction to the arithmetic–harmonic permeability closure for rectangular grooves**

**取舍理由**：
- 三个都不用 "first"、"novel"、"unprecedented"。
- 推荐版把"对象（rectangular imprint grooves）+ 动作（systematic error）+ 交付（closure）"三件事说全，检索友好。
- 备选 A 带问号，PoF 接受但偏少见；备选 B 把"verified"放前面容易被追问"验到什么程度"，而我们的跨代码验证尚未做（§3.6），故不首选。

### 2.3 Abstract（240 词，符合 ≤250 限制）

> Jet-and-flash nanoimprint lithography fills nanoscale grooves by pressing a patterned mold into a thin resist film. Modeling a printed field requires replacing several thousand sub-resolution grooves with an effective permeability tensor, and the closure conventionally used for line-and-space patterns is the arithmetic-harmonic average of the cubic conductance. It is a one-dimensional construction applied to a two-dimensional cross-section: it omits the shear on the groove side walls and the dissipation where the flow contracts into and leaves a groove. We quantify both omissions by solving the steady Stokes flow in explicitly resolved rectangular grooves with the lattice Boltzmann method, over groove depths of 25 to 120 nm and residual gaps of 25 to 125 nm at a 240 nm period, extracting the along- and across-groove permeabilities from the flux driven by a body force. In all 36 cases of the error map the closure overestimates transport, and the error grows monotonically with groove depth, reaching 205% along the grooves and 54% across them. At the reference geometry the closure predicts an anisotropy ratio of 3.70 where the resolved flow gives 2.21, so it misrepresents both the magnitude of transport and the directionality that sets the filling front. The two errors have distinct geometric origins, one scaling with groove depth relative to the volume-equivalent height and the other with groove depth relative to its width, which allows each to be removed by a single dimensionless factor. Applied together, the factors reduce the errors to within 17% and 4%, including on a groove depth excluded from the fit.

**自查**：240 词 ✓｜单段 ✓｜无引用 ✓｜无显示公式 ✓｜无图表 ✓｜无 "first" ✓

## 六、关于"用哪个 LaTeX/Word 模板"

PoF 提供官方 LaTeX 模板（`aip-pof` 类）与 Word 模板。两点实操建议：

1. **LaTeX**：用官方 `revtex4-2` + `aip` 选项，公式编号与双栏自动处理。我们的公式量大（9 个显示公式），LaTeX 更省事。
2. **Word**：AIP 提供 `.dotx`。注意 PoF 2012 年后改为**单栏、单倍行距**，旧的"双栏"模板不要用。

**无论哪种，投稿时需另附一份 PDF 供审稿**，且**图不能嵌在正文里**——AIP 要求图单独上传（TIFF/EPS/PDF），正文只留 `FIG. 1.` 图注位置。

## 七、一句话总结

我们的稿子在**内容和诚实度**上已经超过多数投稿（有完整验证、有负面记录、有明确适用边界），差的全是**格式层**：罗马数字编号、TABLE/FIG 大写、必需的声明三项、以及一份已经写好的 240 词摘要。

**最需要提防的不是格式，而是网上那些"投稿技巧"**——特别是让你在摘要里写 "this is the first study" 的，照做会直接毁掉查新成果。
