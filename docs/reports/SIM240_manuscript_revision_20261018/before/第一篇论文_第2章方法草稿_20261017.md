## II. PROBLEM STATEMENT AND METHODS

### A. Geometry and scope ／ 几何与范围

### EN

The computational domain is one period of an infinite line-and-space pattern, shown in FIG. 1(b) and 1(c). A rigid mold carries rectangular ribs of width $P-w$ separated by grooves of width $w$; the ribs stand a distance $h$ above a flat, rigid substrate, so the groove depth measured from the rib face is $d$. The pattern repeats with period $P$ along $x$, and the grooves extend without bound along $y$. The resist is treated as a Newtonian liquid of density $\rho$ and dynamic viscosity $\mu$ that completely fills the available volume — the film of thickness $h$ beneath the ribs and the groove volume of depth $h+d$ — so that the calculation contains no gas phase and no free surface.

The geometry is non-dimensionalized internally by the period, and two ratios fix the problem: the dimensionless gap $h/P$ and the groove aspect ratio $d/w$. All results are reported for the imprint-relevant period $P=240$ nm and groove width $w=120$ nm (so the duty cycle is $w/P=0.5$), with $h$ and $d$ varied over the ranges given in Sec. II E. The reference geometry used for illustration throughout the paper is $h=75$ nm, $d=100$ nm, for which $h/P=0.3125$.

Because the gap is five orders of magnitude smaller than the lateral extent of a printed field, inertia is negligible and the flow is in the Stokes regime. The Reynolds number based on gap height and the largest computed velocity does not exceed $10^{-3}$, and the Mach number of the lattice Boltzmann solution is below $4\times10^{-7}$ throughout (Sec. II F). The calculation therefore represents the quasi-static limit of the imprint process: the gap is held fixed and the steady flow driven by a prescribed pressure gradient is computed, rather than the transient squeeze that closes the gap. Justification for this separation is given in section 2.2.

### 中文

计算域取无限长线槽图案的一个周期，见 FIG. 1(b) and 1(c)。刚性模具上带有宽度为 $P-w$ 的凸台，凸台之间是宽度为 $w$ 的槽；凸台底面距平刚性基板 $h$，故自凸台底面量起的槽深为 $d$。图案沿 $x$ 以周期 $P$ 重复，槽沿 $y$ 方向无限延伸。胶体视为密度 $\rho$、动力黏度 $\mu$ 的牛顿流体，**完全充满**可用体积——凸台下方厚度为 $h$ 的薄层，以及深度为 $h+d$ 的槽内体积——因此计算中**不含气相、不含自由液面**。

几何在程序内部以周期无量纲化，问题由两个比值确定：无量纲间隙 $h/P$ 与槽的深宽比 $d/w$。全部结果均在压印相关周期 $P=240$ nm、槽宽 $w=120$ nm（占空比 $w/P=0.5$）下报告，$h$ 与 $d$ 在 Sec. II E 给出的范围内变化。全文用于示意的基准几何为 $h=75$ nm、$d=100$ nm，对应 $h/P=0.3125$。

由于间隙比一个曝光场的横向尺度小五个数量级，惯性可忽略，流动处于 Stokes 区。以间隙高度与最大计算速度定义的 Reynolds 数不超过 $10^{-3}$；格点 Boltzmann 解的马赫数全程低于 $4\times10^{-7}$（见 Sec. II F）。因此本计算对应压印过程的**准静态极限**：固定间隙，计算在给定压力梯度驱动下的稳态流动，而非闭合间隙的瞬态挤压过程。这一分离的依据见 Sec. II B。

### B. Governing equations and the quasi-static decomposition ／ 控制方程与准静态分解

### EN

At the scale of a printed field, the resist film obeys the Reynolds lubrication equation with an effective permeability tensor. Writing $H$ for the local gap, $\mathbf{q}$ for the gap-averaged flux per unit width and $\nabla_\parallel$ for the in-plane gradient,

$$
\frac{\partial H}{\partial t} + \nabla_\parallel\cdot\mathbf{q} = 0,
\qquad
\mathbf{q} = -\frac{\mathbf{K}}{\mu}\,\nabla_\parallel p ,
\tag{3}
$$

which is the form used in imprint modeling [2,4] and is the two-scale reduction of the Stokes equations for a thin film.

Equation (3) separates the problem into two parts. The tensor $\mathbf{K}(h,d)$ depends only on the local cross-section, while the evolution of the gap $H(t)$ and of the pressure field is set by the imposed closing motion and the boundary conditions at the liquid front. The present work determines $\mathbf{K}$ and nothing else: $h$ and $d$ are held fixed, the flow is driven by a uniform body force, and the steady state is computed. This is not an approximation to the filling process but a decomposition of it, and it is the standard route in this literature — the permeability is tabulated once as a function of geometry, then used inside a transient lubrication solver [2,4,5]. A direct simulation of the closing gap would require resolving a moving boundary and a free surface, and would not separate the geometric error of $\mathbf{K}$ from the errors of the moving-boundary treatment.

In the calculations the pressure gradient is imposed as a body force. A uniform acceleration $\mathbf{a}$ acting on a liquid of density $\rho$ is equivalent to a pressure gradient $\mathbf{G}=\rho\mathbf{a}$, so that

$$
\mathbf{J} = \frac{\mathbf{K}}{\mu}\,\mathbf{G}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
K = \frac{\mu\,J}{G} = \frac{\mu\,J}{\rho\,|\mathbf{a}|},
\tag{4}
$$

where $\mathbf{J}$ is the flux per unit width obtained by integrating the velocity over the fluid volume and dividing by the plan area. With $\rho=1000$ kg m⁻³ and $|\mathbf{a}|=10^{5}$ m s⁻², the imposed gradient is $G=10^{8}$ Pa m⁻¹, which produces gap-averaged velocities of order $10^{-4}$ m s⁻¹ and keeps the lattice Mach number far below its stability limit. Since $\mathbf{K}$ is extracted from a linear constitutive relation, its value is independent of the magnitude of $\mathbf{G}$ provided the flow remains in the Stokes regime; this is verified in Sec. II F.

### 中文

在曝光场的尺度上，胶膜服从带有效渗透率张量的 Reynolds 润滑方程。记局部间隙为 $H$、单位宽度通量为 $\mathbf{q}$、面内梯度为 $\nabla_\parallel$：

$$
\frac{\partial H}{\partial t} + \nabla_\parallel\cdot\mathbf{q} = 0,
\qquad
\mathbf{q} = -\frac{\mathbf{K}}{\mu}\,\nabla_\parallel p ,
\tag{3}
$$

这正是压印建模中使用的形式 [2,4]，也是薄膜 Stokes 方程的双尺度约化。

式 (3) 把问题分成两部分：张量 $\mathbf{K}(h,d)$ 只取决于局部横截面；间隙 $H(t)$ 与压力场的演化则由外加闭合运动和液面前沿的边界条件决定。本文**只确定 $\mathbf{K}$**：固定 $h$ 与 $d$，以均匀体力驱动，求稳态。这不是对填充过程的近似，而是对它的**分解**，也是本领域通行做法——渗透率先作为几何的函数制表，再放进瞬态润滑求解器 [2,4,5]。若直接模拟闭合间隙，需处理移动边界与自由液面，反而无法把 $\mathbf{K}$ 的几何误差与移动边界处理的误差分开。

计算中压力梯度以体力形式施加。密度为 $\rho$ 的液体受均匀加速度 $\mathbf{a}$ 作用，等效于压力梯度 $\mathbf{G}=\rho\mathbf{a}$，故

$$
\mathbf{J} = \frac{\mathbf{K}}{\mu}\,\mathbf{G}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
K = \frac{\mu\,J}{G} = \frac{\mu\,J}{\rho\,|\mathbf{a}|},
\tag{4}
$$

其中 $\mathbf{J}$ 为单位宽度通量，由速度在流体体积上积分再除以平面面积得到。取 $\rho=1000$ kg m⁻³、$|\mathbf{a}|=10^{5}$ m s⁻²，则外加梯度 $G=10^{8}$ Pa m⁻¹，产生的间隙平均速度量级为 $10^{-4}$ m s⁻¹，格点马赫数远低于稳定上限。由于 $\mathbf{K}$ 由线性本构关系提取，只要流动仍在 Stokes 区，其值与 $\mathbf{G}$ 的大小无关——这一点在 Sec. II F 中检验。

### C. Lattice Boltzmann formulation ／ 格点 Boltzmann 格式

### EN

The steady flow in Eq. (3) is solved directly in the resolved geometry with the lattice Boltzmann method (LBM), using OpenLB 1.8r1 [12]. The D3Q19 velocity set with the force term is used, and the collision operator is the single-relaxation-time BGK model with a body-force correction (ForcedBGK). The evolution equation for the distribution function $f_i$ is

$$
f_i(\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{c}_i\Delta t,\, t+\Delta t)
= f_i(\mathbf{x},t)
- \frac{1}{\tau}\left[f_i(\mathbf{x},t)-f_i^{\rm eq}(\mathbf{x},t)\right]
+ \Delta t\,F_i ,
\tag{5}
$$

where $\tau$ is the relaxation time, $f_i^{\rm eq}$ the equilibrium distribution and $F_i$ the forcing term accounting for $\mathbf{a}$. The kinematic viscosity follows from the relaxation time as

$$
\nu = c_s^2\left(\tau-\tfrac12\right)\Delta t ,
\qquad c_s^2=\tfrac13\frac{\Delta x^2}{\Delta t^2},
\tag{6}
$$

so that with the grid spacing $\Delta x$ fixed by the geometry, $\Delta t$ and $\tau$ are tied by the requirement $\nu=10^{-6}$ m² s⁻¹. The choice $\tau=1$ is used throughout: it places the solution away from the stability boundary at $\tau\to\tfrac12$, and it is a standard choice for steady Stokes flow, where accuracy is limited by the boundary treatment rather than by the collision model. The corresponding time step is $\Delta t=4.1667\times10^{-12}$ s and the lattice viscosity is $\nu^\ast=\nu\Delta t/\Delta x^2=1/6$.

Solid walls are treated with the interpolated bounce-back scheme of Bouzidi, Firdaouss and Lallemand [13], as implemented in OpenLB. The wall position is supplied analytically: the fluid indicator computes the intersection of every lattice link with the exact groove profile, rather than rasterizing the geometry onto the grid.

This matters here for a quantitative reason. The grid spacing $\Delta x=5$ nm divides every value of $h$ and $d$ used in this study, so in every computed case each wall lies exactly halfway between a fluid node and a solid node — a fractional distance $q=1/2$ from the fluid node. The code enforces this by snapping round-off-sized deviations of the computed intersection to exactly $q=1/2$, so that OpenLB selects the same branch of the interpolated scheme in every cell. At $q=1/2$ the scheme reduces to the elementary half-way bounce-back, which reproduces a planar, axis-aligned no-slip wall without displacing it.

The alternative, a staircase (voxel) representation, would place the wall on the nearest node rather than half a cell away, displacing every surface by $\Delta x/2=2.5$ nm. For the reference geometry that is a 3.3% error in $h$ and a 2.5% error in $d$; for the thinnest gap computed, $h=25$ nm, it would be a 10% error. An error of that size in the geometry is comparable to the closure error the paper sets out to measure at the shallow end of the matrix, and it would enter as a systematic bias correlated with $h$ — precisely the variable whose effect is being quantified. The combination of analytic wall location and $q=1/2$ landing therefore does not improve the order of accuracy; it removes a geometric error that would otherwise be indistinguishable from the effect under study.

### 中文

式 (3) 的稳态流动在**显式几何**中直接用格点 Boltzmann 方法（LBM）求解，程序为 OpenLB 1.8r1 [12]。速度集为带力项的 D3Q19，碰撞算子为带体力修正的单松弛时间 BGK（ForcedBGK）。分布函数 $f_i$ 的演化方程为

$$
f_i(\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{c}_i\Delta t,\, t+\Delta t)
= f_i(\mathbf{x},t)
- \frac{1}{\tau}\left[f_i(\mathbf{x},t)-f_i^{\rm eq}(\mathbf{x},t)\right]
+ \Delta t\,F_i ,
\tag{5}
$$

其中 $\tau$ 为松弛时间，$f_i^{\rm eq}$ 为平衡态分布，$F_i$ 为体现 $\mathbf{a}$ 的力项。运动黏度由松弛时间给出：

$$
\nu = c_s^2\left(\tau-\tfrac12\right)\Delta t ,
\qquad c_s^2=\tfrac13\frac{\Delta x^2}{\Delta t^2},
\tag{6}
$$

于是网格步长 $\Delta x$ 由几何确定后，$\Delta t$ 与 $\tau$ 由 $\nu=10^{-6}$ m² s⁻¹ 这一要求绑定。全文取 $\tau=1$：该值使解远离 $\tau\to\tfrac12$ 处的稳定边界，也是稳态 Stokes 流动的标准取值——此类问题中精度受边界处理而非碰撞模型限制。对应时间步 $\Delta t=4.1667\times10^{-12}$ s，格点黏度 $\nu^\ast=\nu\Delta t/\Delta x^2=1/6$。

固壁采用 Bouzidi–Firdaouss–Lallemand 插值反弹格式 [13]（OpenLB 实现）。壁面位置以**解析方式**给出：流体指示函数计算每条格点连线与精确槽型的交点，而不是把几何栅格化到网格上。

此处采用它有一个**定量**理由。网格步长 $\Delta x=5$ nm 整除本研究用到的每一个 $h$ 与 $d$ 取值，因此每个算例中每一面壁都恰好位于流节点与固节点的正中——距流节点的分数距离 $q=1/2$。程序把求交结果的舍入级偏差吸附到严格 $q=1/2$，以确保 OpenLB 在每个格点选用同一分支。在 $q=1/2$ 处，该格式退化为基本的半步反弹，可**不偏移地**复现平面、轴向对齐的无滑移壁面。

若改用阶梯（体素）表示，壁面会被放到最近节点而非半格处，所有表面各偏移 $\Delta x/2=2.5$ nm。对基准几何，这是 $h$ 的 3.3%、$d$ 的 2.5% 误差；对最薄的 $h=25$ nm 档，则是 **10%** 误差。这一量级的几何误差与本文在矩阵浅端要测量的闭合误差相当，且会以**与 $h$ 相关的系统偏差**形式混入——而 $h$ 恰恰是被量化的变量。因此"解析定壁 + 落在 $q=1/2$"并不提高精度阶，而是**消除一项否则会与研究效应混同的几何误差**。

### D. Domain, discretization, and boundary conditions ／ 计算域、离散与边界条件

### EN

The domain is one period in $x$ and $y$, discretized with $\Delta x=5$ nm, giving $48\times48$ nodes per period and a working resolution of $h/\Delta x=15$ at the reference geometry. The number of periods is therefore one in each direction, which is legitimate because the pattern is periodic and the flow is driven uniformly: a single period with periodic boundary conditions is an exact representation, not an approximation. Periodicity is imposed on all three velocity components and on the density at the $x$ and $y$ faces. The substrate and the mold surfaces are no-slip, imposed with the interpolated bounce-back scheme described above. In the $z$ direction the domain is closed by the substrate at $z=0$ and by the mold surface at $z=h$ over the ribs and $z=h+d$ over the grooves; there is no free surface and no inflow or outflow boundary, since the driving is volumetric.

The resolution was chosen from two competing requirements. The groove depth must be resolved finely enough to represent the corner region, where the dissipation that the paper quantifies is generated; and the total cost must allow the full geometric matrix, including the holdout and extension batches, to be computed. At $\Delta x=5$ nm the reference geometry has 15 cells across the gap and 20 across the groove depth. The sensitivity of the extracted coefficients to this choice, and the confirmation that the discretization is in its asymptotic range, are quantified in Sec. II F.

Steady state is declared when the flux has converged in a window of 1000 consecutive steps, with the relative standard deviation of both the streamwise and the cross-stream flux below $10^{-6}$ and the relative span below $5\times10^{-6}$, and the run is stopped at 60 000 steps if this is not reached. In practice all reported cases converged well within the limit.

### 中文

计算域在 $x$、$y$ 方向各取一个周期，以 $\Delta x=5$ nm 离散，每周期 $48\times48$ 个节点，基准几何下工作分辨率为 $h/\Delta x=15$。因此每个方向只有**一个周期**；这是合法的，因为图案周期、驱动均匀，**单周期加周期边界是精确表示而非近似**。三个速度分量与密度在 $x$、$y$ 两对面均施加周期性。基板与模具表面为无滑移，按上节的插值反弹格式施加。$z$ 方向由 $z=0$ 的基板与模具表面封闭——凸台下方 $z=h$、槽内 $z=h+d$；由于驱动是体积力，域内**没有自由液面，也没有进出流边界**。

分辨率由两个相互竞争的要求决定：槽深必须细到足以刻画角部区域（本文量化的耗散在此产生），而总计算量必须允许完成整个几何矩阵（含留出考场与扩展批次）。在 $\Delta x=5$ nm 下，基准几何沿间隙 15 个网格、沿槽深 20 个网格。该取值对提取系数的影响、以及离散确实处于渐近区间的确认，在 Sec. II F 中定量给出。

稳态判据为：通量在连续 1000 步的窗口内收敛，流向与横向通量的相对标准差均低于 $10^{-6}$、相对极差低于 $5\times10^{-6}$；若未达到，则在 60 000 步时停止。实际所有报告算例均远早于此收敛。

### F. Verification and numerical uncertainty ／ 验证与数值不确定度

> ⚠ **PoF 结构提示**：本节内容与第 3 章重复。定稿建议压缩为一句并指向 Sec. III，届时本节删除、G. 前移为 F.。

### EN

Four independent checks establish that the extracted coefficients are numerically converged and physically correct. They are reported separately because they test different things, and none of them substitutes for another.

**(i) Parallel-plate limit.** With the grooves removed ($d=0$) the geometry degenerates to two parallel plates and $K$ is known exactly, $K_0=h^3/12$. Three grids give relative errors of $0.444\%$, $0.111\%$ and $0.0272\%$ at $\Delta x=5$, $2.5$ and $1.25$ nm, giving successive error ratios of $4.00$ and $4.08$ — i.e. clean second-order convergence. This is the behaviour expected for a planar wall at $q=1/2$, where the boundary treatment introduces no displacement error; it also shows that the bulk discretization of the parabolic profile is second order. At the finest grid the error is essentially at the level of that bulk discretization.

**(ii) Grid convergence on the patterned geometry.** A three-grid study at the reference geometry gives a Richardson-extrapolated convergence order and a Grid Convergence Index that are collected in TABLE II. For the two principal components the fine-grid GCI is $0.125\%$ for $K_\parallel$ and $0.551\%$ for $K_\perp$, and $0.465\%$ for their ratio. The observed order is below two for both components, and lowest for $K_\perp$ ($p=1.256$). The pattern is consistent with the difference between the two geometries rather than with a defect of the boundary scheme: in the parallel-plate case, where the same code and the same boundary treatment return $p=1.991$ and a fine-grid GCI of $0.035\%$, the solution is a parabola and the wall is a straight line. In the patterned geometry the solution has an interior singularity at the groove corners, where the wall-normal velocity gradient is discontinuous; the error there is dominated by the corner neighbourhood, which converges more slowly than the bulk and pulls the apparent order down. This is a property of the geometry, not of the discretization, and it is why the GCI rather than the formal order is used to bound the uncertainty — and why the grid-refinement study in check (iii) is reported alongside it.

| $K_\perp$ | 1.256 | $4.3394\times10^{-23}$ | 0.551 |

**(iii) Resolution sensitivity of the reported coefficients.** Repeating two of the matrix geometries at $\Delta x=2.5$ nm — the deepest groove at the smallest gap ($h=50$, $d=100$ nm) and the shallowest groove at the largest gap ($h=125$, $d=25$ nm), i.e. the two extremes of the matrix — changes $K$ by $0.33\%$ and $0.52\%$ for the streamwise component and by $0.72\%$ and $2.16\%$ for the cross-stream component. The cross-stream coefficient is the more sensitive of the two, as expected: it is controlled by the step dissipation at the groove mouth, which is the feature most affected by the corner discretization. These changes are an order of magnitude smaller than the closure errors reported in Sec. IV, which is the relevant comparison.

**(iv) In-run gates.** Every case must satisfy, at steady state: mass drift below $10^{-10}$ relative; maximum density deviation below $10^{-6}$; maximum lattice Mach number below $0.05$; agreement between the fluxes across two interior sections within $10^{-5}$ relative; vanishing of the cross-stream flux within $10^{-12}$; and invariance of the material distribution. All 48 cases passed. The measured Mach numbers are of order $10^{-7}$ and the mass drift is at round-off level (typically reported as exactly zero), confirming that the compressibility error of the LBM is negligible at these velocities — which matters because the method is only formally incompressible in the limit of vanishing Mach number.

The residual numerical uncertainty in $\mathbf{K}$ is therefore below about $0.6\%$ for the streamwise component and $2.2\%$ for the cross-stream component, against closure errors of 10–205% (Sec. IV). Numerical error is thus not a limiting factor in any conclusion of this paper, and the error map reported below is a property of the closure, not of the discretization.

### G. From coefficients to the closure comparison ／ 从系数到闭合对比

### EN

Two normalization choices must be stated explicitly, because the comparison with Eq. (1) is only meaningful if both sides are defined the same way.

First, the volume-equivalent height. For a partly grooved cross-section the natural reference height is not the gap $h$ but the height of the uniform film holding the same liquid volume per unit plan area,

$$
H_{\rm ref} = \frac{V}{A_{\rm plan}} = h + \varphi d ,
\qquad \varphi=\frac{w}{P},
\tag{7}
$$

with $\varphi=0.5$ for the present geometry. $H_{\rm ref}$ therefore varies with each geometry — from $62.5$ nm to $175$ nm across the matrix — and is computed pointwise rather than fixed at the reference value of $125$ nm.

Second, the closure of Eq. (1) is evaluated with the same $h$ and $d$ as the simulation and compared to the computed $K$ through the relative error

$$
E=\frac{K^{\rm mod}-K^{\rm DNS}}{K^{\rm DNS}},
\tag{8}
$$

reported separately for each principal direction. Because both $K^{\rm mod}$ and $K^{\rm DNS}$ are computed for the same geometry, the same driving and the same normalization, $E$ isolates the error of the closure itself. It absorbs neither a difference in the definition of $K$ nor a difference in the reference height, and it does not depend on the viscosity (Sec. IV).

Equation (1) is reproduced here in the form used for comparison, with $\varphi=1/2$ evaluated explicitly:

$$
K_\parallel^{\rm mod}=\frac{1}{24}\left[(h+d)^3+h^3\right],
\qquad
K_\perp^{\rm mod}=\frac{1}{6}\,\frac{(h+d)^3h^3}{(h+d)^3+h^3}.
\tag{9}
$$

The two expressions are the arithmetic and harmonic averages of the cubic conductance over the period, and the prefactors $1/24$ and $1/6$ follow from the parallel-plate conductance $h^3/12$ combined with the $1/2$ weighting of the two halves of the period. They are the same construction as the closed forms used in imprint modeling [2,4], written per unit width.

### 中文

有两处归一化必须明确交代，因为只有两侧定义一致，与式 (1) 的比较才有意义。

**其一，体积等效高度。** 对部分开槽的横截面，自然参考高度不是间隙 $h$，而是"容纳相同单位面积液量"的均匀膜厚：

$$
H_{\rm ref} = \frac{V}{A_{\rm plan}} = h + \varphi d ,
\qquad \varphi=\frac{w}{P},
\tag{7}
$$

本几何 $\varphi=0.5$，故 $H_{\rm ref}$ 随每种几何而变——矩阵内从 $62.5$ nm 到 $175$ nm——按**逐档计算**，不固定为基准点的 $125$ nm。

**其二，闭合关系与误差定义。** 式 (1) 用与仿真相同的 $h$、$d$ 求值，并通过相对误差与计算值比较：

$$
E=\frac{K^{\rm mod}-K^{\rm DNS}}{K^{\rm DNS}},
\tag{8}
$$

两个主方向分别报告。由于 $K^{\rm mod}$ 与 $K^{\rm DNS}$ 针对同一几何、同一驱动、同一归一化，$E$ 隔离的正是**闭合关系自身**的误差；它既不含 $K$ 定义口径的差异，也不含参考高度的差异，且与黏度无关（Sec. IV）。

式 (1) 按对比所用形式重写如下，其中 $\varphi=1/2$ 已显式代入：

$$
K_\parallel^{\rm mod}=\frac{1}{24}\left[(h+d)^3+h^3\right],
\qquad
K_\perp^{\rm mod}=\frac{1}{6}\,\frac{(h+d)^3h^3}{(h+d)^3+h^3}.
\tag{9}
$$

两式分别是立方导通率在一个周期上的算术平均与调和平均；前置系数 $1/24$、$1/6$ 由平行平板导通率 $h^3/12$ 与周期两半各占 $1/2$ 的权重合成。它们与压印建模中使用的闭式解 [2,4] 属同一构造，此处按单位宽度写出。
