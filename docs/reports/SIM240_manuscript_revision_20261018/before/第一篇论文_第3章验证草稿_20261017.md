## III. VERIFICATION

### A. Verification strategy ／ 验证策略

### EN

The credibility of an error map such as the one reported in section 5 rests entirely on whether the coefficients in it are trustworthy. We therefore separate the checks into four layers, each answering a different question, and none of them substitutable for another:

| Layer | Question | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I. Analytic limit** | Does the code solve the right equation? | Degenerate the geometry to a case with an exact solution | Sec. III C: error 0.027–0.44%, second order |
| **II. Discretization** | How large is the numerical error at the production grid? | Three-grid Richardson extrapolation, GCI, spot refinement | Sec. III D: GCI 0.13–0.55%; refinement 0.33–2.2% |
| **III. Physical consistency** | Does the extracted quantity obey the laws it must? | Tensor rotation law, viscosity independence, linear response | Sec. III E: 6/6 within $10^{-4}$%, $\mu$-independent to 0.0006% |
| **IV. Data chain** | Are the numbers in the paper the numbers the code produced? | Independent recomputation from raw output, no shared code | Sec. III F: 0 discrepancies in 120 quantities |

Two things are deliberately reported as **not** established, and are stated here rather than left for a referee to discover: one of the nine grid-convergence runs fails one gate by 20% (Sec. III D 3), and no cross-code comparison against an independent solver has been performed (Sec. III G).

第 5 章那张误差地图是否可信，完全取决于其中的系数是否可信。因此本文把检查分成**四层**，每层回答不同的问题，且互不可替代：

| 层 | 问题 | 方法 | 结果 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I. 解析极限** | 代码解的是不是正确的方程？ | 把几何退化到有精确解的情形 | Sec. III C：误差 0.027–0.44%，二阶 |
| **II. 离散** | 生产网格上的数值误差有多大？ | 三网格 Richardson 外推、GCI、点加密 | Sec. III D：GCI 0.13–0.55%；加密 0.33–2.2% |
| **III. 物理自洽** | 提取的量是否遵守它必须遵守的规律？ | 张量旋转律、黏度无关性、线性响应 | Sec. III E：6/6 在 $10^{-4}$% 内；黏度无关至 0.0006% |

### B. Test configurations ／ 测试设置

### EN

Three configurations are used, chosen so that each isolates one aspect of the numerical error.

**Smooth75** is the parallel-plate limit: the groove is removed ($d=0$), leaving two flat plates separated by $h=75$ nm. The exact mobility is $K_0=h^3/12=3.515625\times10^{-23}$ m³, so this case tests the bulk solver and the wall treatment against a closed-form answer with no geometric complication.

**EX240_parallel and EX240_perpendicular** are the reference patterned geometry ($h=75$ nm, $d=100$ nm) driven along and across the grooves. These carry the corner singularity and the step, so they are the cases the grid study must convince us about.

All three are computed on the same three grids, $\Delta x=5$, $2.5$ and $1.25$ nm. The nominal volume and the number of fluid nodes are held consistent by the analytic geometry indicator, so that the grid sequence compares discretizations of the same physical domain rather than slightly different ones.

### 中文

采用三套配置，使每一套隔离数值误差的一个侧面。

**Smooth75** 是平行平板极限：去掉沟槽（$d=0$），只留间距 $h=75$ nm 的两块平板。精确解为 $K_0=h^3/12=3.515625\times10^{-23}$ m³，故该算例在无任何几何复杂性的条件下，以闭式解检验体相求解器与壁面处理。

**EX240_parallel 与 EX240_perpendicular** 是基准带图案几何（$h=75$ nm、$d=100$ nm），分别沿槽与跨槽驱动。它们携带角点奇异与台阶，正是网格研究必须说服我们的对象。

三者均在相同三套网格上计算：$\Delta x=5$、$2.5$、$1.25$ nm。解析几何指示函数保证三套网格的名义体积与流体节点数一致，从而使网格序列比较的是**同一物理域的不同离散**，而非略有差别的域。

### C. Layer I: the analytic limit ／ 层 I：解析极限

### EN

On Smooth75 the computed mobility is, at the three grids,

| $\Delta x$ (nm) | $K$ (m³) | $K_0$ (m³) | Relative error | Error ratio |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | $3.531250\times10^{-23}$ | $3.515625\times10^{-23}$ | $0.444\%$ | — |
| 2.5 | $3.519529\times10^{-23}$ | $3.515625\times10^{-23}$ | $0.111\%$ | 4.00 |
| 1.25 | $3.516580\times10^{-23}$ | $3.515625\times10^{-23}$ | $0.0272\%$ | 4.08 |

The error falls by a factor of four for each halving of the grid, which is the signature of a second-order scheme. This result deserves a precise interpretation, because it is easy to over-claim from it.

The second-order behaviour here is the behaviour of the *bulk* discretization applied to a parabolic velocity profile, together with a wall that lies exactly halfway between nodes ($q=1/2$, section 2.3). At $q=1/2$ the interpolated bounce-back reduces to the elementary half-way bounce-back, which introduces no wall displacement, and the error is then controlled entirely by the interior scheme. This is why the observed order is two. It does **not** mean that the interpolated bounce-back scheme is globally second order for walls at arbitrary fractional distances — OpenLB documents its linear-interpolation variant as first order, and nothing in the present calculations depends on that capability (section 2.3). The conclusion we draw is therefore narrow and defensible: for a straight, axis-aligned wall at $q=1/2$, the code reproduces the closed-form mobility to $0.44\%$ at the production grid and converges at second order under refinement.

在 Smooth75 上，三套网格的计算迁移率为：

|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | $3.531250\times10^{-23}$ | $3.515625\times10^{-23}$ | $0.444\%$ | — |
| 2.5 | $3.519529\times10^{-23}$ | $3.515625\times10^{-23}$ | $0.111\%$ | 4.00 |
| 1.25 | $3.516580\times10^{-23}$ | $3.515625\times10^{-23}$ | $0.0272\%$ | 4.08 |

网格每加密一倍，误差降至约四分之一——这是二阶格式的标志。但这个结果需要**精确解读**，因为它很容易被过度引申。

此处的二阶行为，是**体相离散**作用于抛物型速度剖面、并配合一面**恰位于节点正中**（$q=1/2$，§2.3）的壁面所呈现的行为。在 $q=1/2$ 处，插值反弹退化为基本半步反弹，不引入壁面位移，误差便完全由内点格式控制——这才是观测阶为二的原因。它**并不**意味着插值反弹格式对任意分数距离的壁面都是整体二阶（OpenLB 把其线性插值变体记为一阶），而本文的计算也不依赖那一能力（§2.3）。因此我们得到的结论**窄而可守**：对 $q=1/2$ 的平直轴向壁，代码在生产网格上以 $0.44\%$ 复现闭式迁移率，并在加密下二阶收敛。

All six quantities are in the asymptotic range (ratio of the two GCI values close to unity, between 0.994 and 1.004), which justifies using the extrapolated values as the error estimates.

The apparent orders for the two patterned cases are below two, and lowest for $K_\perp$ ($p=1.256$). The pattern is informative rather than troubling. In the parallel-plate case the solution is a parabola and the wall a straight line, and the same code returns $p=1.991$. In the patterned geometry the solution has an **interior singularity at the groove corners**: on one side of a corner the wall is parallel to the flow, on the other it is perpendicular, so the wall-normal velocity gradient jumps. The local error is therefore dominated by the corner neighbourhood, which converges more slowly than the bulk, and the apparent order of the integral quantity is dragged below the bulk order. This is a property of the geometry rather than a defect of the scheme, and it is why the GCI, and not the formal order, is used to bound the uncertainty — and why the independent refinement check in Sec. III D 4 is reported alongside.

#### 3. One gate not passed: recorded as it is ／ 一处未通过的门禁：如实记录

#### EN

One of the nine runs does not pass its verification gate, and we state this explicitly rather than rounding it away.

The run is EX240_perpendicular at $\Delta x=1.25$ nm. It converges (29548 steps, window criteria met) and passes every gate except mass conservation: the maximum relative mass drift reaches $1.197\times10^{-10}$, against a tolerance of $10^{-10}$. The exceedance is 20%. Every other gate on that run is satisfied by a wide margin — maximum density deviation $1.7\times10^{-9}$, maximum Mach number $3.1\times10^{-8}$, section-to-section flux difference $5.0\times10^{-9}$, unresolved lattice links zero.

The failure is understood and is a property of the test rather than of the solution. Mass drift grows monotonically with grid refinement across the sequence, from exactly zero at $\Delta x=5$ nm, through $8.5\times10^{-12}$ at $2.5$ nm, to $1.2\times10^{-10}$ at $1.25$ nm — roughly an order of magnitude per halving. The number of time steps grows as $\Delta x^{-2}$ (2940, 8699, 29548 in the three runs), so the accumulated round-off in the mass sum grows with the step count, while the tolerance was set at a fixed $10^{-10}$ for the production grid. At $1.25$ nm the run executes ten times as many steps as at $5$ nm, and the drift crosses the fixed threshold. In absolute terms the drift corresponds to a relative density error of order $10^{-10}$, which is at the level of double-precision accumulation over $3\times10^{4}$ updates.

We have deliberately left this record as a failure rather than reclassifying it. Two consequences follow, and we state both. First, the fine-grid $K_\perp$ value enters the GCI calculation above, so the uncertainty estimate for $K_\perp$ is computed from a run that did not pass all gates; the effect is bounded, since the other five quantities in TABLE IV come from runs that did pass, and the GCI for $K_\perp$ (0.551%) is itself small. Second, the production grid used for the whole error map, and for the two supplementary sets as well, is $\Delta x=5$ nm, at which the mass drift is zero or of order $10^{-13}$ and every gate passes; the adequacy of that grid is therefore not affected by this record. The relevant question for the paper is whether $5$ nm is fine enough, and that is answered by Sec. III C and Sec. III D 4, not by the fine-grid gate.

这一失败可以解释，且属于**测试本身的性质而非解的性质**。质量漂移沿网格序列单调增大：$\Delta x=5$ nm 时恰为零，$2.5$ nm 时 $8.5\times10^{-12}$，$1.25$ nm 时 $1.2\times10^{-10}$——每加密一倍约涨一个数量级。时间步数按 $\Delta x^{-2}$ 增长（三档分别为 2940、8699、29548 步），质量求和的累积舍入随步数增长，而容差当初是按生产网格设定的固定值 $10^{-10}$。在 $1.25$ nm 下执行的步数是 $5$ nm 下的十倍，漂移便越过了这一固定阈值。折成绝对量，该漂移对应 $10^{-10}$ 量级的相对密度误差，相当于在 $3\times10^{4}$ 次更新上双精度累积的水平。

我们**特意把这条记录保留为失败**，而不重新归类。由此产生两个后果，一并说明。其一，最细网格的 $K_\perp$ 值参与了上述 GCI 计算，故 $K_\perp$ 的不确定度估计来自一个未过全部门禁的算例；影响是有界的——TABLE IV 中其余五个量来自全部通过门禁的算例，且 $K_\perp$ 的 GCI 本身只有 0.551%。其二，整个误差地图（以及两个补充集）所用的生产网格是 $\Delta x=5$ nm，该网格上质量漂移为零或 $10^{-13}$ 量级、全部门禁通过，故**生产网格的适用性不受本条记录影响**。本文真正要回答的问题是"5 nm 够不够细"，这由 Sec. III C 与 Sec. III D 4 回答，而不是由最细网格的门禁回答。

### E. Layer III: physical consistency ／ 层 III：物理自洽性

### EN

Three independent physical statements are tested. Each is a relation that the computed $\mathbf{K}$ must satisfy if the extraction and the solver are both correct, and each is checked against a prediction that does not come from the simulation itself.

**(i) The tensor rotation law.** If $\mathbf{K}$ is a rank-two tensor, then a groove rotated by $\theta$ relative to the driving direction must return $K(\theta)=K_\parallel\cos^2\theta+K_\perp\sin^2\theta$, and the flow must be deflected by $\phi$ with $\tan\phi=(K_\perp/K_\parallel)\tan\theta$. Six cases — $\theta=30°$, $45°$, $60°$ at two geometries — reproduce both relations with the parameters $K_\parallel$ and $K_\perp$ taken from the *unrotated* simulations, i.e. with no free parameter. Agreement is better than $10^{-4}$% in all six. At $\theta=45°$ the computed deflection is $24.3424°$ and the predicted value $24.3424°$; the four-figure agreement is not a fit.

**(ii) Independence from viscosity.** If $\mathbf{K}$ is a purely geometric quantity, raising $\mu$ must leave $\mathbf{K}$ unchanged while scaling the flux in inverse proportion. Repeating the reference geometry with the kinematic viscosity raised fifteen-fold, $\nu=1.5\times10^{-5}$ m² s⁻¹ ($\mu=15$ mPa s, the order of a commercial high-viscosity imprint resist), changes $K_\parallel$ by $0.0006\%$ and $K_\perp$ by $0.0003\%$, while the flux falls by exactly the factor fifteen (ratio 15.000). This is the quantitative justification for treating $\mathbf{K}$ as a geometric property in section 2.2, and it is what allows the error map to be carried over to any Newtonian resist without recomputation.

**(iii) Linearity of the response.** The extraction uses $K=\mu J/(\rho a)$, which presumes the flux is linear in the driving. Because the Mach number stays at $3\times10^{-7}$ or below in every case, the flow is in the incompressible limit by three orders of magnitude, and the lattice Boltzmann solution is in its linear regime. The gate on the cross-stream flux — which must vanish when the drive is aligned with a principal axis — is met to $2\times10^{-22}$ m³ s⁻¹ in the worst case, confirming that the driving produces no spurious transverse response.

### 中文

检验三条独立的物理陈述。每一条都是"若提取方式与求解器均正确，则计算出的 $\mathbf{K}$ 必须满足"的关系，且每一条都对照一个**不来自本次仿真**的预测进行核查。

**(i) 张量旋转律。** 若 $\mathbf{K}$ 是二阶张量，则相对驱动方向旋转 $\theta$ 后必须给出 $K(\theta)=K_\parallel\cos^2\theta+K_\perp\sin^2\theta$，且流向偏转满足 $\tan\phi=(K_\perp/K_\parallel)\tan\theta$。六个算例——两种几何下的 $\theta=30°$、$45°$、$60°$——在 $K_\parallel$、$K_\perp$ **取自未旋转仿真**（即无任何自由参数）的条件下同时复现两式，六个全部优于 $10^{-4}$%。$\theta=45°$ 时计算偏转角 $24.3424°$、预测值 $24.3424°$；四位有效数字相符不是拟合出来的。

**(ii) 与黏度无关。** 若 $\mathbf{K}$ 是纯几何量，提高 $\mu$ 必须使 $\mathbf{K}$ 不变、同时使流量按反比缩放。在基准几何下把运动黏度提高十五倍（$\nu=1.5\times10^{-5}$ m² s⁻¹，即 $\mu=15$ mPa·s，商品高黏压印胶的量级），$K_\parallel$ 变化 $0.0006\%$、$K_\perp$ 变化 $0.0003\%$，而流量恰按 15 倍下降（比值 15.000）。这既是 §2.2 把 $\mathbf{K}$ 当作几何性质处理的定量依据，也是误差地图可以不经重算而适用于任意牛顿胶的原因。

**(iii) 响应的线性。** 提取采用 $K=\mu J/(\rho a)$，前提是通量对驱动线性。由于所有算例中马赫数均在 $3\times10^{-7}$ 以下，流动处于不可压极限三个数量级之内，格点 Boltzmann 解处在其线性区间。横向通量的门禁——驱动沿主轴时必须消失——最差情形满足到 $2\times10^{-22}$ m³ s⁻¹，确认驱动未引发虚假的横向响应。

### G. What was not done ／ 未做的验证：如实说明

### EN

Two gaps remain, and stating them is more useful than letting a referee find them.

**Cross-code comparison.** No independent solver was run on this geometry. The verification above is internal: it establishes that this code solves this equation correctly on this grid, not that a different discretization of the Navier–Stokes equations would return the same $K$. A comparison against a body-fitted finite-element solution at one or two geometries would close this gap, and is the natural next step; it is not claimed here.

**Cross-literature reproduction.** We have not reproduced an independent published solution on a geometry where one exists. The natural candidate is the spectral theory for *sinusoidal* grooves [8,9], which would test the solver against an external analytic result rather than against an internal consistency relation. This was not done because the sinusoidal geometry is not the one of interest and would have required a separate geometry implementation. The reader should therefore regard the verification as establishing numerical control of the present calculations, not as a validation against independent simulation.

What the four layers do establish is the following, and we state it narrowly: the code reproduces a closed-form mobility to 0.44% at the production grid; the discretization contributes at most 2.2% to the reported coefficients, against closure errors of 10–205%; the extracted tensor obeys the rotation law to $10^{-4}$% and is independent of viscosity to $10^{-5}$; and every reported number traces to a raw solver output through an audited chain.

### 中文

还有两处缺口，把它们说出来比留给审稿人发现更有用。

**跨代码比对未做。** 本文没有在同一几何上运行独立求解器。上述验证是**内部**的：它确立的是"本代码在本网格上正确求解本方程"，而不是"Navier–Stokes 方程的另一种离散会给出同样的 $K$"。在一到两种几何上与贴体有限元解比对可以补上这一缺口，这是自然的下一步，但本文**不作此声称**。

**跨文献复现未做。** 我们没有在有独立已发表解的几何上做复现。自然的候选是**正弦槽**的谱理论 [8,9]——它能以外部解析结果（而非内部自洽关系）检验求解器。未做的原因是正弦几何并非本文关心的对象，需要另做一套几何实现。因此读者应把本章的验证理解为"确立了本次计算的数值可控性"，而**不是**"与独立仿真完成了确认"。

四层验证**确实**确立的是以下内容，我们窄口径地表述：代码在生产网格上以 0.44% 复现闭式迁移率；离散对报告系数的贡献最多 2.2%，而闭合误差为 10%–205%；提取的张量满足旋转律至 $10^{-4}$%、与黏度无关至 $10^{-5}$；且每个报告数字都经审计链条追溯到原始求解器输出。

## 写作说明（不进正文）

### 一、本章的五个写作决策

**① 开头就摆出四层表，并把两处负面记录提前"认领"。**

审稿人最不信任的是"报喜不报忧"。Sec. III A 的表格后面紧跟一句"有两件事特意作为未确立报告"，读者一进门就知道我们没藏东西。这比把问题埋到附录里更能建立信任。

**② Sec. III D 3 单独设节讲那一处 FAIL，写清"为什么失败""影响多大""为什么生产网格不受影响"。**

这是全章最需要拿捏的部分。处理原则：
- **不美化**：明写 PASS=false、超出 20%、"保留为失败而不重新归类"；
- **讲清机理**：步数按 $\Delta x^{-2}$ 增长 → 累积舍入增长 → 固定阈值被越过；
- **划清影响范围**：只影响 $K_\perp$ 的 GCI 输入，不影响生产网格；
- **给出替代依据**：生产网格的适用性由平板极限 0.444% 与敏感性 ≤2.16% 独立支撑。

**③ Sec. III C 主动限制自己的结论。**

平板极限跑出漂亮二阶，很容易顺手写"本格式二阶精度"。但 OpenLB 官方标 Bouzidi 为一阶。所以写的是"这是 $q=1/2$ 平面壁的行为，**不**意味着插值反弹全局二阶"。**主动收窄**能避免被审稿人抓，反而显得专业。

**④ Sec. III E 三条物理检验都强调"预测不来自本次仿真"。**

旋转律用**未旋转**的 $K_\parallel$、$K_\perp$ 作参数，黏度无关性用 Darcy 关系的反比预测，横向通量用"驱动沿主轴应为零"。这三条都是**外部可预测**的，不是自己拟合自己。

**⑤ Sec. III G 明写两个"未做"。**

跨代码比对、跨文献复现（正弦槽）都没做。写出来并说明"这是自然的下一步"，比让审稿人自己发现强。
