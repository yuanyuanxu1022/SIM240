                    We show that discrete lattice effects must be considered in the introduction of a force into the lattice
                 Boltzmann equation. A representation of the forcing term is then proposed. With the representation, the
                 Navier-Stokes equation is derived from the lattice Boltzmann equation through the Chapman-Enskog expan-
                 sion. Several other existing force treatments are also examined.
