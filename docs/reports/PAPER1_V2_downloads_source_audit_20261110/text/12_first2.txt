                                               pe
                in the design of a micromixer geometry. Due to computational power limi-
                tations, the accurate tracking of the fluid structures that increase this area
                has been impossible until recently. The design process of the micromixers
                was based on the estimation of the geometrical Dean number, which serves
                as a characteristic number for the secondary flow. The mixing efficiency was
