### ⚠ 一个必须避开的"通用建议"陷阱

网上有中文投稿攻略建议："摘要结尾需明确标注 `To the best of our knowledge, this is the first study to...` 以凸显原创性"。

**这条对我们有害，绝对不做。** 理由：查新任务 C（WoS 被引列表）未完成，"首次"在我们项目里是**禁写词**（见范围文档 §1 与引言草稿的措辞纪律）。照抄这句话等于把两轮查新的成果一次性作废。

### 2.3 Abstract（证据约束修订稿）

> Effective transport models for nanoimprint lithography replace unresolved grooves with an anisotropic permeability closure. We assess the arithmetic–harmonic local-cubic-law closure using steady, fully filled Newtonian flow in ideal rectangular grooves. At a 240 nm period and 120 nm width, the map contains 18 sampled geometries and 36 principal-direction calculations, spanning gaps of 25–125 nm and depths of 25–120 nm, not every combination. The closure overestimates the computed transport coefficients by 10.30–204.85% along the grooves and 8.11–54.19% across them. These errors propagate: a uniform-closing model underestimates the squeeze load by 38.8% at the median and 62.9% at worst, and the drainage time by up to 59.5%; the correction reduces these to 6.3% and 4.2%. At the reference geometry it predicts an anisotropy ratio of 3.70 rather than 2.21, and a 9.20° flux-angle error under 45° forcing. Geometry-specific refinement and consistency checks support the comparisons but do not establish a full-map uncertainty bound. The correction is written in the groove aspect ratio and fitted on nine geometries. With published rounded coefficients the maximum absolute residual is 10.5% along the grooves and 6.9% across them at gaps of at least 50 nm, with a −18.7% outlier at 25 nm across. Model selection followed access to extension results; these evaluations are retrospective, not independent prediction tests or continuous-domain guarantees. Side-wall shear and step redistribution remain candidate mechanisms, not separated contributions. The results concern fixed-gap, no-slip transport and do not validate dynamic filling or universal transfer to real resists.

## 六、关于"用哪个 LaTeX/Word 模板"

PoF 提供官方 LaTeX 模板（`aip-pof` 类）与 Word 模板。两点实操建议：

1. **LaTeX**：用官方 `revtex4-2` + `aip` 选项，公式编号与双栏自动处理。我们的公式量大（9 个显示公式），LaTeX 更省事。
2. **Word**：AIP 提供 `.dotx`。注意 PoF 2012 年后改为**单栏、单倍行距**，旧的"双栏"模板不要用。

**无论哪种，投稿时需另附一份 PDF 供审稿**，且**图不能嵌在正文里**——AIP 要求图单独上传（TIFF/EPS/PDF），正文只留 `FIG. 1.` 图注位置。

## 七、一句话总结

当前完成的是三章及摘要的证据约束修订，不是投稿准备完成。参考解不确定度、来源链、修正预测验证及作者确认仍待补齐，不能把剩余工作概括为只有格式。期刊规则是旧查阅记录，正式投稿前须重新核对。

**最需要提防的不是格式，而是网上那些"投稿技巧"**——特别是让你在摘要里写 "this is the first study" 的，照做会直接毁掉查新成果。
