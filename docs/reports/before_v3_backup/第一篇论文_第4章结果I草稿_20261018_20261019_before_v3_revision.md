## IV. RESULTS I: THE RESOLVED COEFFICIENTS

### EN

#### A. What the solved flow gives

We report first the resolved coefficients themselves, before any comparison with a closure. Two quantities are needed, $K_\parallel$ (transport along the grooves, driven across $x$) and $K_\perp$ (transport across the grooves, driven across $y$), obtained from Eq. (4) at steady state for the thirty-six geometries of TABLE I. Both are quoted as $K$ in m³, consistent with the per-unit-width definition of Sec. II G; conversion to the three-dimensional permeability used in porous-media and heat-pipe practice requires division by the period, $K^{[\mathrm{m^2}]}=K^{[\mathrm{m^3}]}/P$.

**FIG. 4** shows the result. Panel (a) gives $K_\parallel$ and $K_\perp$ against groove depth $d$ for each of the four gaps investigated; panel (b) gives their ratio.

#### B. The two components depend on different variables

The most useful observation from FIG. 4 is not the magnitude of either coefficient but the distinct way each responds to the geometry.

**The across-groove coefficient is almost independent of groove depth.** Over the whole range $d=25$–$120$ nm, at fixed gap, $K_\perp$ changes by less than 4%: 1.037× at $h=25$ nm, 1.034× at 50 nm, 1.021× at 75 nm and 1.009× at 125 nm. The curves in panel (a) that correspond to $K_\perp$ are, to plotting accuracy, horizontal. What sets $K_\perp$ is the gap $h$ alone, and it does so strongly: $K_\perp$ rises by a factor of $87$ from $2.14\times10^{-24}$ m³ at $h=25$ nm to $1.86\times10^{-22}$ m³ at $h=125$ nm.

**The along-groove coefficient depends on both.** $K_\parallel$ rises with $d$ at fixed $h$, and the rise is itself gap-dependent: from $d=25$ to $120$ nm at $h=50$ nm it grows by a factor of $3.55$, at $h=75$ nm by $2.13$, and at $h=125$ nm by only $1.39$. So the groove adds transport capacity along its axis chiefly when the gap above it is thin.

This asymmetry has a direct physical reading, and it is worth stating plainly because it organises everything that follows. **Transport across the grooves must cross the groove mouth**, where the flow contracts into the groove on one side and expands out of it on the other. The resistance of that contraction–expansion pair is a property of the mouth, not of the volume behind it: a deeper groove does not make the mouth any wider. **Transport along the grooves runs inside the groove**, so its conductance is set by the groove cross-section and therefore grows with $d$.

#### C. The anisotropy is a gap effect, not a depth effect

Panel (b) renders the same statement as a ratio. Across the thirty-six geometries the anisotropy spans $A=1.090$ to $15.45$, a factor of fourteen. It is not the depth alone that produces this: at fixed $d=100$ nm, $A$ falls from $15.45$ at $h=25$ nm to $2.210$ at $h=75$ nm and $1.432$ at $h=125$ nm. Conversely, at fixed $h=125$ nm, $A$ rises only from $1.090$ to $1.503$ as $d$ goes from 25 to 120 nm.

Two limits bound the family of curves, and both are physically meaningful.

- **$A\to1$ as $d\to0$.** With no groove the cross-section is a plane channel and the two directions are equivalent. The data approach this limit from above as $d$ becomes small at large gap: $A=1.090$ at $(h,d)=(125,25)$ nm.
- **$A$ grows without apparent bound as $h\to0$ at fixed $d$.** The trend in panel (b) steepens as $h$ decreases, and the smallest gap computed, $h=25$ nm with $d=100$ nm, already gives $A=15.45$. We did not compute below 25 nm; the extrapolation is not made here.

The practical consequence is the one that matters for process design: **the directionality of transport in an imprint groove is controlled by how thin the residual layer is, not by how deep the pattern is.** A designer who wants to reduce directionality — to make the resist flow more evenly in both in-plane directions — has one effective lever, and it is $h$.

#### D. Equivalent-volume efficiency

A quantity that makes the second point quantitative is the efficiency with which a given volume of groove contributes to transport. Let $H_{\rm ref}=h+\varphi d$ be the volume-equivalent height, Eq. (7), with $\varphi=w/P=0.5$. A plane channel of that height would have conductance $H_{\rm ref}^3/12$. The ratio

$$
\eta_{\alpha}=\frac{K_{\alpha}^{[\mathrm{m^3}]}}{H_{\rm ref}^{3}/12},\qquad \alpha=\parallel,\perp
$$

measures how much of the transport capacity implied by the volume is actually delivered.

The result is a clean separation. $\eta_\parallel$ stays between $0.53$ and $0.95$: most of the groove volume still moves liquid along the groove. $\eta_\perp$ collapses, from $0.852$ at $(h,d)=(125,25)$ nm to $0.132$ at $(50,120)$ nm, a factor of $6.5$. At the deepest groove with the thinnest gap computed, **$87\%$ of the volume contributes nothing to cross-groove transport**.

Note that $\eta_\parallel$ is almost independent of $h$ at fixed $d$ (e.g. $0.688$, $0.604$, $0.597$ at $d=100$ nm for the three gaps), whereas $\eta_\perp$ is strongly $h$-dependent. This is the same asymmetry as in Sec. IV B, expressed as a volume efficiency rather than a coefficient.

#### E. Summary of this section

Three statements carry forward to the comparison with the closure.

1. $K_\perp$ is set by $h$ alone and is insensitive to $d$ (variation under 4% over the whole depth range).
2. $K_\parallel$ depends on both, and the contribution of groove depth is largest when the gap is thin.
3. Consequently the anisotropy $A$ is primarily a gap effect: at $d=100$ nm it changes by a factor of $11$ as $h$ goes from 25 to 125 nm, whereas at $h=125$ nm it changes by only $1.4$ over the whole depth range.

These are properties of the resolved flow and are established independently of any closure. Section IV B asks what a one-dimensional closure predicts for the same geometries, and Sec. V explains why the two differ.

## 中文

### A. 解出来的系数

在与任何闭合比较之前，先报告解出来的系数本身。需要两个量：$K_\parallel$（沿槽输运，沿 $x$ 驱动）与 $K_\perp$（跨槽输运，沿 $y$ 驱动），由式 (4) 在稳态下对 TABLE I 的三十六种几何求得。两者均以 m³ 给出，与 Sec. II G 的单位宽度定义一致；若要换算成多孔介质与热管惯用的三维渗透率，需除以周期：$K^{[\mathrm{m^2}]}=K^{[\mathrm{m^3}]}/P$。

**FIG. 4** 给出结果。面板 (a) 是四个间隙下 $K_\parallel$、$K_\perp$ 随槽深 $d$ 的变化；面板 (b) 是两者之比。

### B. 两个分量由不同的变量控制

FIG. 4 最有用的观察不是两个系数的大小，而是它们对几何的**响应方式不同**。

**跨槽系数几乎与槽深无关。** 在 $d=25$–$120$ nm 的全范围内，固定间隙时 $K_\perp$ 的变化小于 4%：$h=25$ nm 时为 1.037 倍，50 nm 时 1.034 倍，75 nm 时 1.021 倍，125 nm 时 1.009 倍。面板 (a) 中对应 $K_\perp$ 的曲线，在作图精度内是水平的。**决定 $K_\perp$ 的只有间隙 $h$，而且影响很强**：$K_\perp$ 从 $h=25$ nm 的 $2.14\times10^{-24}$ m³ 增至 $h=125$ nm 的 $1.86\times10^{-22}$ m³，共 87 倍。

**沿槽系数由两者共同决定。** 固定 $h$ 时 $K_\parallel$ 随 $d$ 上升，而上升幅度本身又依赖间隙：$d$ 从 25 到 120 nm，$h=50$ nm 时增至 3.55 倍，$h=75$ nm 时 2.13 倍，$h=125$ nm 时仅 1.39 倍。**槽对沿槽输运能力的贡献，主要发生在它上方的间隙很薄的时候。**

这种不对称有直接的物理解读，值得讲清楚，因为它组织了后面的一切：**跨槽输运必须穿过槽口**——流体在一侧收缩进入槽内、另一侧扩张流出，这对收缩–扩张的阻力属于**槽口**，而不属于槽口之后的容积，槽再深也不会让槽口变宽。**沿槽输运则在槽内进行**，其导度由槽的截面积决定，因此随 $d$ 增长。

### C. 各向异性是间隙效应，不是深度效应

面板 (b) 把同一事实画成比值。三十六种几何中 $A$ 从 1.090 到 15.45，跨 14 倍。但这不是单靠深度造成的：固定 $d=100$ nm，$A$ 从 $h=25$ nm 的 15.45 降到 $h=75$ nm 的 2.210、再到 $h=125$ nm 的 1.432；反过来，固定 $h=125$ nm，$d$ 从 25 到 120 nm 时 $A$ 只从 1.090 升到 1.503。

两个极限界定了这一族曲线，且都有物理意义：

- **$d\to0$ 时 $A\to1$。** 没有槽，截面就是平行平板，两个方向等价。数据从上方趋近该极限——大间隙小槽深处 $A=1.090$（$h=125$、$d=25$ nm）。
- **固定 $d$、$h\to0$ 时 $A$ 持续增大且未见上界。** 面板 (b) 中 $h$ 越小趋势越陡；已算的最小间隙 $h=25$ nm 配 $d=100$ nm 时 $A$ 已达 15.45。**25 nm 以下未计算，此处不作外推。**

对工艺设计而言，最重要的推论是：**压印沟槽中输运的方向性由残膜有多薄控制，而不由图案有多深控制。** 若设计者想降低方向性——让胶在面内两个方向流得更均匀——**唯一有效的杠杆是 $h$。**

### D. 等体积效率

把第二点定量化的量，是"给定的槽容积有多少真正转化为输运能力"。设 $H_{\rm ref}=h+\varphi d$ 为等体积高度（式 7），$\varphi=w/P=0.5$；同高度的平行平板导度为 $H_{\rm ref}^3/12$。比值

$$
\eta_{\alpha}=\frac{K_{\alpha}^{[\mathrm{m^3}]}}{H_{\rm ref}^{3}/12},\qquad \alpha=\parallel,\perp
$$

即衡量"由容积所隐含的输运能力有多大比例真正兑现"。

结果分离得很干净。$\eta_\parallel$ 一直在 0.53–0.95 之间：槽容积的大部分仍在沿槽方向推动液体。$\eta_\perp$ 则塌陷，从 $(h,d)=(125,25)$ nm 的 0.852 降到 $(50,120)$ nm 的 0.132，差 6.5 倍。在已算的最深槽配最薄间隙处，**87% 的容积对跨槽输运毫无贡献**。

### E. 本章小结

三条结论将带入与闭合的比较：

1. $K_\perp$ 只由 $h$ 决定，对 $d$ 不敏感（全深度范围内变化小于 4%）。
2. $K_\parallel$ 由两者共同决定，且槽深的贡献在间隙薄时最大。
3. 因此各向异性 $A$ 主要是间隙效应：$d=100$ nm 时 $h$ 从 25 到 125 nm 使 $A$ 变化 11 倍，而 $h=125$ nm 时整个深度范围内只变化 1.4 倍。

这些都是**解出来的流动本身**的性质，其成立与任何闭合无关。第 5 章将问：同样这些几何上，一维闭合会预测出什么；第 6 章解释两者为何不同。

## 写作说明

### 一、本章的定位

**这一章刻意不做对比。** 第 5 章才谈闭合误差。分开的好处：

1. 第 4 章的数字是"真实值"，可以独立成立、独立被引用；
2. 第 5 章的误差表有了干净基准，不必在同一个图里混两类信息；
3. 审稿人若质疑闭合，不会连带质疑 K 本身。

### 二、本章的主线：不是"K 有多大"，而是"两个分量由不同变量控制"

这是数据自己长出来的结论（$K_\perp$ 在 4% 内不随 $d$ 变），不是我预设的框架。它比"报告一张大表"有力得多，因为：

- 它解释了为什么后面可以用**一个**变量（$d/w$）去拟合两个修正因子；
- 它给出了"该调什么参数"的直接答案；
- 它是第 5 章修正式形式的物理依据。

### 三、PPT 里那句"深槽 82% 容积是死空间"的出处

这个说法来自 $\eta_\perp$。但要精确：

| 说法 | 精确值 | 位置 |

| "深槽 82% 容积是死空间" | $\eta_\perp=0.176$ → 82.4% | $(h,d)=(50,100)$ nm |
| PPT/论文若用 87% | $\eta_\perp=0.132$ → 86.8% | $(50,120)$ nm（$d/w=1$，边界档） |

**建议统一用 $(50,100)$ 的 82%**，因为那一档在标定域内；120 是边界档。
